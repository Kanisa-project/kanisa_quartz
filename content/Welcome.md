## Texioty
[[Texioty]] is made up of two parts, [[texoty]] and [[texity]]. Texity allows you to input commands, while texoty shows you output from any [[TexHelpers|helper]] interaction. The first commands to learn and understand are the 'welcome' 'commands' 'help' ones.
