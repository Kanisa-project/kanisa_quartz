Boston trail is a lot like the Oregon Trail but the other direction. You'll need to travel, hunt and gather across America from Seattle to Boston.


### Engine
Starting place is far from the target ending. Move and survive, just not too fast nor too slow. Resting and hunting areas are good to travel from gathering to hunting and continuing.

### Profile
The starting place has to be east or west coast, and the target ending spot is on the opposite coast. You need to gather bear necessities like water and food, and there are a lot of food options.

### Commands

| Command |                  Description                  |
| :-----: | :-------------------------------------------: |
| travel  |     Travel a certain length of distance.      |
|  hunt   | Hunt for a specific animal for some supplies. |
| gather  |      Gather some supplies you're low on.      |
