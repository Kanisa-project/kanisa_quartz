

Candy slinger is a game of slinging candy around town. You can buy anything that is available from the area you're in or sell any of your pieces in your inventory. Moving between areas can spark a random event, but you'll need to make as much money as you can.

### Engine
The engine is pretty simple, a thing has a price at a location. There is a way to map any thing to a candy and any location exists in a town. The higher your profit, the better your score.

### Profile
A profile is how someone can map any thing to a candy, change the pricing of candies, add or remove locations, and probably more.


### Commands

| Command |                       Short Description                        |
| :-----: | :------------------------------------------------------------: |
|  move   |    Doesn't matter where you were, only where you're going.     |
|   buy   |   You gotta buy something in order to sell that some thing.    |
|  sell   | If you earned it, you own it, if you own it, you can trade it. |
