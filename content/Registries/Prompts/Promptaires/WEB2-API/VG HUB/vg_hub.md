vg_hub is where you can find game related promptaires.

## VGs
Video games like [[osrs|Runescape]], [[arc_raiders|ARC Raiders]] or even [[starcraft|Starcraft]] have different items and missions or quests. These promptaires are meant to get any kind of information available by the API.

## Hub
A hub being a central part of the activity of different video games. This is how you'd find high scores, items, quests or even some tips/strategies from the wiki.
