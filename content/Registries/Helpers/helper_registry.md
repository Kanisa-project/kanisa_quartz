  [[TexHelpers]] is what allows Texioty to do what a Texioty does, they are also the pre-version of [[widget_registry|Widgets]] for a more visually accelerated experience (it adds images).

## TUI helpers
   TUI helpers are Text-based User Interfaces that allow a [[Texioty]] environment to navigate creating or viewing digital assets. Containing only text elements, these kinds of helpers mainly ask questions and await responses. 


##  GUI helpers
   GUI helpers are Graphical User Interfaces that enhance a Texioty environment, also called [[Widgets]]
