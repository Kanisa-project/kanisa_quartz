Kanisa project was created to contain [[kanisaShell]]. Here is a short background of the word.

## Kenisa combo
I wanted a new term for my project, and for a while I was reading about the Elder Futhark runes. I liked the idea of a creative fire (Kenaz) being frozen (Isa) to create unique artwork. "Kenisa" didn't feel too right, so I changed the 'e' to an 'a' and got "Kanisa". 

## Kenaz rune
The K-rune (Kenaz) symbolizes fire controlled by human; a torch, a forge, a hearth or hot air balloon is all about creating and exploring. Destroying something with fire can be seen as transforming old into new.

## Isa rune
The I-rune (Isa) represents ice or the absence of heat, which creates a duality from the first part. This duality got me thinking about limits and the limits of those limits.


## Texioty
[[Texioty]] is made up of two parts, [[texoty]] and [[texity]]. Texity allows you to input commands, while texoty shows you output from any [[TexHelpers|helper]] interactions. The first commands to learn and understand are: '[[welcome]]' '[[commands]]' '[[help]]'.
